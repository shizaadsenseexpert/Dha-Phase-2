Location & Access Guide

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad — Location, Map & Distance Guide

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad sits in Zone 5 of the Capital Development Authority (CDA), in the Kahuta Triangle between two of the twin cities' most important road arteries — GT Road (N-5) on the western and southern boundary, and the Islamabad Expressway (N-80) on the northern and eastern boundary. This prime positioning makes it one of the most strategically accessible addresses in the Islamabad–Rawalpindi region, with seamless access to both cities without inner-city congestion.

Route Guide

How to reach DHA Phase 2 Islamabad

From Islamabad (City Center / Zero Point)

Take the Islamabad Expressway heading towards Rawalpindi. Continue past Faizabad and follow signs for DHA. Take the DHA Phase 2 exit and enter via Gate 5 or Gate 7.

From Rawalpindi (Saddar / Committee Chowk)

Take GT Road (N-5) heading towards Islamabad. DHA Phase 2 will appear on your right side. Enter via Gate 1 (near Giga Mall), Gate 2 (Iqbal Boulevard), Gate 3, or Gate 4.

From New Islamabad International Airport

Take the Islamabad Expressway heading towards Rawalpindi. Follow the expressway until the DHA Phase 2 exit. Enter via Gate 5 or Gate 7. Total drive time approximately 25–30 minutes.

From Bahria Town Rawalpindi

Exit Bahria Town onto GT Road heading towards Islamabad. DHA Phase 2 Gate 1 (near Giga Mall) is approximately 5–8 minutes away on the left side.

How far is DHA Phase 2 from Islamabad city center?
