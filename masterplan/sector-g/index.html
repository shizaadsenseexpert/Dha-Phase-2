Overview

About Sector G

Central Boulevard is the busiest road inside Phase 2, and Sector G is built along it. The boulevard is lined with well-known brands such as McDonald's and Pizza Hut, plus banks, cafés and shops, so the sector stays lively from morning until late at night.

Families are drawn by the DHA Army Public School campus, three mosques and the Central Park near the boulevard. The sector also contains the Phase 2 graveyard, one of the community's essential public facilities.

Sector G is part of Phase 2's central apartment corridor, with projects such as Central Palace Residence offering apartments with on-site shops, restaurants, gyms and sports facilities, often on instalment plans.

Location & access: Sector G lies on Central Boulevard, which links to Gate 4 on GT Road through Jinnah Boulevard and runs through the middle of Phase 2 towards the Expressway gates. That makes it one of the easiest sectors to reach from any direction.

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector G

Sector G is part of the central high-rise corridor of Phase 2. Central Palace Residence is the best-known project here, with varied apartment layouts, on-site shops, restaurants, gyms and sports facilities, and instalment-based payment options through the developer.

Commercial plazas along Central Boulevard add more apartments above shops and office floors. These are popular with tenants who want to be close to food outlets, banks and the main road.

At a glance

  • Central Palace Residence: apartments with shops, gyms and sports facilities
  • Instalment plans available through the developer
  • Boulevard plazas with upper-floor apartments and offices
