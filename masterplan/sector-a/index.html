DHA Phase 2 Sector A Islamabad

Sector A is one of the most mature and commercially active sectors in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad. Fully developed markets, bank branches, three mosques and quick access to the Lignum Tower corridor make it a top choice for buyers who want shops, services and apartments within walking distance of home.

Overview

About Sector A

Sector A was among the first parts of Phase 2 to be fully settled, and today it has the feel of a complete town centre. Most streets are built up with modern double-storey houses, the sector market is busy from morning to late evening, and almost every daily need, from banking and groceries to pharmacies and cafés, is a short walk away.

For investors, Sector A stands out because of its rental demand. Its markets and the nearby Lignum Tower corridor attract working professionals and small families who want an address that is convenient and secure. Houses and apartments here rarely sit empty for long.

Because the sector is fully developed, there is little open land left to build on. Most transactions are resale of houses or of the few remaining plots, which keeps prices firm and at the upper end of the Phase 2 range.

Location & access: Sector A connects to the main internal boulevards that run between the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8). Residents commuting to Rawalpindi usually use the GT Road gates, while those heading to Islamabad use the Expressway side.

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector A

Sector A sits next to the Lignum Tower corridor, which gives residents quick access to its studio to 4-bedroom apartments without leaving the area. Lignum Tower itself rises 20 storeys and offers high-speed lifts, standby power, covered parking, a gym and CCTV security.

Inside the sector, the commercial market is lined with multi-storey plazas. These buildings usually have shops on the ground floor and offices or apartments on the upper floors, which makes them one of the busiest rental pockets in Phase 2 for singles, couples and small families.

At a glance

  • Lignum Tower apartments (studio to 4-bed) next door
  • Multi-storey commercial plazas with upper-floor apartments
  • Office floors above shops, popular with small businesses

Buyer's Verdict

Pros

Things to consider

  • Among the higher prices in Phase 2

FAQs

Sector A FAQs

Is Sector A good for investment?

Yes. Its commercial market and closeness to Lignum Tower keep rental demand high, and limited open land keeps resale prices firm.

What plot sizes are available in Sector A?

Mainly 10 Marla and 1 Kanal, with some 8 Marla plots and 4–5 Marla commercial plots in the sector market.

Does Sector A have high-rise apartments?

It has multi-storey plazas with apartments on upper floors and is right next to the Lignum Tower corridor, which offers studio to 4-bedroom units.

How many mosques are in Sector A?

Three mosques, each built for more than 300 worshippers.
