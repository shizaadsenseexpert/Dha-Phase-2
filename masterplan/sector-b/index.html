Family Living & Sports

DHA Phase 2 Sector B Islamabad

Sector B is built around family life. It is home to one of the largest open green areas in DHA Phase 2, the DHA Tennis Court and a popular food strip, which makes it one of the most in-demand sectors for families with children.

Overview

About Sector B

Life in Sector B revolves around its large family park. In the mornings it fills with walkers and joggers, in the evenings with families and children, and on weekends it becomes a social hub for the whole sector. Few other sectors in Phase 2 offer this much open green space so close to home.

Next to the park is the DHA Tennis Court, plus a strip of well-known local eateries such as Zahoor Café and Snack Shack, and a sector shopping mall for everyday needs. Together they give Sector B a relaxed, community feel without needing to drive out for weekend plans.

Most of the sector is independent houses rather than apartments, so streets are quieter and less crowded than in the commercial sectors. That low density, plus the park, is why houses here hold their value well and sell quickly.

Location & access: Sector B is linked to Phase 2's main boulevard network, giving quick routes to the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) for Rawalpindi and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8) for Islamabad.

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector B

Sector B has no tower-style high-rise buildings. Living here is almost entirely independent houses, which is a big part of its appeal for families who want privacy and a quiet street.

A limited number of flats are available above shops in the sector shopping mall and food strip. These suit students, young couples and small families who want to live near the park on a smaller budget. For full high-rise living, Lignum Tower and the Central Boulevard projects are a short drive away.

At a glance

  • No tower-style high-rises
  • Small flats above shops in the sector mall
  • Nearest towers: Lignum Tower and Central Boulevard projects

Buyer's Verdict

  • Quiet, low-density streets

Things to consider

FAQs

Sector B FAQs

Why is Sector B popular with families?

Its large family park, tennis court, eateries and quiet streets make it ideal for family life.

Are there apartments in Sector B?

There are no high-rise towers, only a limited number of flats above shops in the sector mall.

What is the price of a 10 Marla plot in Sector B?

Roughly 2.7 Cr to 3.4 Cr in 2025, depending on location and facing.

Is there a sports facility in Sector B?

Yes, the DHA Tennis Court is in Sector B, next to the family park.
