Overview

About Sector H

Sector H is one of the quieter parts of Phase 2. Most of the sector is family houses on calm streets, with sector marts and mosques covering everyday needs.

It also has a number of guest houses and the ISR Boys Hostel, which create steady demand for short-stay and shared accommodation. For investors, that means rental opportunities beyond standard family tenancies.

Because Sector H is further from the big commercial hubs, prices are lower than in the central sectors. A 5 Marla plot here starts from around 1.5 Cr, one of the lowest entry points into DHA Phase 2.

Location & access: Sector H is connected to Phase 2's internal boulevard network, with routes to the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8).

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector H

Sector H is low-rise and has no apartment towers. Guest houses and the ISR Boys Hostel are the main forms of shared or short-stay accommodation in the sector.

Buyers who want a full apartment can look at Lignum Tower in Sector C or the Central Boulevard projects in Sector G, both a short drive away.

At a glance

  • No high-rise buildings
  • Guest houses for short stays
  • ISR Boys Hostel
  • Nearest apartments in Sectors C and G

FAQs

Sector H FAQs

Is Sector H affordable?

Yes. It is one of the most affordable sectors in Phase 2, with 5 Marla plots from around 1.5 Cr.

What is ISR Boys Hostel?

A boys' hostel located in Sector H that provides shared accommodation.

Are there apartments in Sector H?

No. Sector H is low-rise; accommodation options are houses, guest houses and the hostel.

What plot sizes are available?

5 Marla, 8 Marla, 10 Marla and 1 Kanal.
