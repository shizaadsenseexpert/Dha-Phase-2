Giga Mall & GT Road Frontage

DHA Phase 2 Sector F Islamabad

Sector F is the most self-contained sector in DHA Phase 2. It sits at Gate 1 on GT Road, right next to Giga Mall, and has its own schools, mosques and commercial area. Its GT Road frontage also holds the most valuable commercial land in the whole community.

Overview

About Sector F

Giga Mall, one of the largest malls in the twin cities, sits at the edge of Sector F next to Gate 1. For residents that means international brands, a food court, a cinema and family entertainment within minutes, and for investors it means some of the strongest commercial demand in Phase 2.

Sector F is also complete in itself. It has two schools, including an APS campus, two mosques, a sector commercial area, banks and parks. Many residents rarely need to leave the sector for daily needs.

The GT Road frontage is the most expensive land in Phase 2. Commercial plots here are bought by brands, banks, developers and long-term investors, and prices can reach 26–34 Cr for 1 Kanal.

Location & access: Sector F has direct access through Gate 1 on GT Road, next to Giga Mall, making it the quickest sector to reach from Rawalpindi and Bahria Town. The internal boulevards connect it to the Expressway gates for Islamabad.

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector F

Sector F's skyline is defined by the Giga Mall complex and the GT Road commercial belt. Multi-storey buildings here mix retail on the lower floors with offices and apartment floors above, and the Giga Mall Extension has added more vertical space to the area.

It is the go-to sector for buyers who want a high-rise address next to a major mall, and for investors who want commercial units or offices with GT Road visibility. Inside the sector, residential streets remain low-rise family houses.

At a glance

  • Giga Mall complex and Giga Mall Extension
  • GT Road commercial high-rises with office and apartment floors
  • Low-rise family houses inside the sector

Buyer's Verdict

Pros and cons of Sector F

Pros

  • Next to Giga Mall
  • Own schools, mosques and market
  • Direct Gate 1 access from GT Road
  • Highest-value commercial land in Phase 2

Things to consider

  • GT Road and mall traffic at peak times
  • Residential prices at the top of the range
  • Commercial plots need a very large budget
Best for: Families who want a self-contained sector next to a major mall, Rawalpindi commuters, and commercial investors seeking GT Road frontage.

Sector F FAQs

Is Giga Mall in DHA Phase 2?

Yes. Giga Mall is next to Gate 1 on GT Road, at the edge of Sector F.

GT Road facing commercial plots in Sector F, with 1 Kanal plots at roughly 26–34 Cr.

Does Sector F have schools?

Yes, two schools including a DHA Army Public School campus.

Which gate is closest to Sector F?

Gate 1 on GT Road, next to Giga Mall.
