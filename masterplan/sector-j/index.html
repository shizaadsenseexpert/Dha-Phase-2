Overview

About Sector J

Sector J is one of the smallest sectors in Phase 2. It is purely residential, with family marts and mosques for everyday needs and quiet streets of family homes.

Its compact size is part of the appeal: there is very little through-traffic and neighbours tend to know each other. Bigger facilities such as schools, Giga Mall, JFC and Central Boulevard are a short drive away in other sectors.

Sector J shares the lowest price band in Phase 2 with Sector H, which makes it a practical way to get a DHA address, build your own home and benefit from DHA security and infrastructure on a tighter budget.

Location & access: Sector J connects to Phase 2's internal boulevards, with routes to the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8).

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector J

Sector J has no high-rise buildings or plaza apartments. It is a quiet street-house sector, best suited to buyers who want to build their own home at an affordable DHA price.

The nearest apartment options are Lignum Tower in Sector C and the Central Boulevard projects in Sector G.

At a glance

  • No high-rise buildings
  • Independent family houses only
  • Nearest apartments in Sectors C and G

FAQs

Sector J FAQs

Is Sector J the cheapest sector in DHA Phase 2?

Sector J and Sector H share the lowest price band, with 5 Marla plots from around 1.5 Cr.

What facilities are in Sector J?

Family marts, mosques and pocket parks. Schools and malls are a short drive away.

Are there high-rises in Sector J?

No. Sector J is a low-rise residential sector.

What plot sizes are in Sector J?

5 Marla, 8 Marla, 10 Marla and 1 Kanal.
