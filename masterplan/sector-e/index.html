Jacaranda Family Club

DHA Phase 2 Sector E Islamabad

Sector E is the recreational heart of DHA Phase 2. It is home to Jacaranda Family Club (JFC), the community's premier leisure destination, and its streets are mostly low-density family homes, which gives the sector a calm and upscale feel.

Overview

About Sector E

Jacaranda Family Club (JFC) is what sets Sector E apart. It is one of the best-equipped family clubs in the twin cities, with a bowling alley, cinema, tennis courts, swimming pool, spa, gym and restaurants, all within a short walk or drive for Sector E residents.

Outside the club, Sector E is mainly 10 Marla and 1 Kanal houses on quiet, tree-lined streets. There are no towers and very little through-traffic, so the sector feels private and peaceful.

Living close to JFC is a strong selling point, especially for families with teenagers and for buyers who entertain often. Plots and houses facing or near the club usually command a premium.

Location & access: Sector E is linked to the main boulevard network inside Phase 2, with routes to both the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8).

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector E

Sector E is a low-rise, villa-style sector with no high-rise towers or plaza apartments. This is a deliberate part of its character: the sector is designed around family homes and the club.

Buyers who want an apartment but still want to use JFC often look at Lignum Tower in Sector C or the Central Boulevard projects in Sector G, both a short drive away.

At a glance

  • No high-rise buildings
  • Low-density family homes only
  • Nearest apartments in Sectors C and G

Sector E FAQs

What is Jacaranda Family Club?

JFC is DHA Phase 2's main family club in Sector E, with bowling, a cinema, tennis, a swimming pool, a spa, a gym and restaurants.

Are there apartments in Sector E?

No. Sector E is a low-rise sector with family houses only.

What plot sizes are in Sector E?

10 Marla and 1 Kanal residential plots.

How much is a 1 Kanal plot in Sector E?

Roughly 4.5 Cr to 6.0 Cr in 2025, with JFC-facing plots at the top of the range.
