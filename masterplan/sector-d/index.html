DHA Phase 2 Sector D Islamabad

Overview

About Sector D

Sector D is often described as the sensible middle ground of Phase 2. It is not as busy as the commercial sectors and not as exclusive as the park-facing streets of Sector B or the JFC side of Sector E, but it has everything a family needs for daily life.

Its sector commercial market covers groceries, pharmacies, tailors, bakeries and small restaurants, while green belts and pocket parks run between the residential blocks. Every plot size from 5 Marla to 1 Kanal is available, giving buyers more choice than in most other sectors.

Because prices are slightly lower than in Sectors A, F and G, Sector D attracts first-time DHA buyers and families upgrading from outside DHA.

Location & access: Sector D is connected to the main boulevard network, with routes to the GT Road gates (Gates 1–4) and the Islamabad Expressway gates (Gates 5, 7 and 8).

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector D

Sector D does not have a signature high-rise tower. Apartment living is available in the multi-storey commercial plazas of the sector market, where flats above shops are popular with small families, couples and tenants.

These plaza apartments are a more affordable alternative to Lignum Tower in neighbouring Sector C, and they let residents live right next to everyday shops.

At a glance

  • No signature tower
  • Flats above shops in sector market plazas
  • Lignum Tower in nearby Sector C

FAQs

Sector D FAQs

Is Sector D cheaper than other sectors?

It is generally a little cheaper than Sectors A, F and G while still being fully developed.

What plot sizes are available in Sector D?

5 Marla, 8 Marla, 10 Marla and 1 Kanal residential plots, plus commercial plots.

Are there apartments in Sector D?

Yes, flats above shops in the sector market plazas. There is no tower.

Does Sector D have parks?

Yes, green belts and pocket parks run through the sector.
