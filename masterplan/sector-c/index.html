Lignum Tower, Schools & Markets

DHA Phase 2 Sector C Islamabad

Sector C combines busy commercial markets with a strong cluster of schools, and is home to Lignum Tower, the landmark 20-storey apartment building near the Islamabad Expressway. It is a practical pick for families who prioritise the school run and for apartment buyers and investors.

Overview

About Sector C

Sector C is best known for Lignum Tower, one of the most recognisable buildings in Phase 2. Its 20 storeys of studio to 4-bedroom apartments are visible from the Islamabad Expressway and have made Sector C the go-to choice for buyers who want a secure, serviced apartment inside DHA.

The sector is also an education hub. It has a DHA Army Public School (APS/APSACS) campus and several private schools, so many families choose Sector C simply to keep the school run to a few minutes. Busy commercial markets add shops, clinics and restaurants within the sector.

Its position on the Expressway side gives residents fast access to Islamabad through Gates 5 and 7, which is a big advantage for people working in the capital.

Location & access: Sector C sits towards the Islamabad Expressway side of Phase 2. Gates 5 and 7 on the Expressway are the quickest entry points from Islamabad, while the internal boulevards connect to the GT Road gates for Rawalpindi.

High-Rises & Apartments

High-rise living in Sector C

Lignum Tower is the headline high-rise in Sector C. It offers 20 storeys of studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4-bedroom luxury apartments with high-speed lifts, standby power backup, covered car parking, a gymnasium, a kids' play area, CCTV security and an intercom system.

Upper-floor units enjoy open views across Phase 2, while lower floors are popular with families who want easy lift access. Apartments here are in demand from overseas Pakistanis, professionals and small families, which supports steady rental income.

Beyond the tower, the sector market has multi-storey plazas with apartments above shops for buyers on a smaller budget.

At a glance

  • High-speed lifts, power backup and covered parking
  • Gym, kids' play area, CCTV and intercom
