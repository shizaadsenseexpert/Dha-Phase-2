Master Plan & Sectors Guide

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad Master Plan: Sectors, Plots & Rates

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad covers 15,075 Kanals between GT Road and the Islamabad Expressway, planned to international urban design standards. The community is divided into nine sectors, A to J, each with its own mix of residential plots, commercial markets, high-rise apartments, mosques, parks and schools. This guide walks through every sector: what you can buy there, what it costs from minimum to maximum, and what facilities are on your doorstep.

Master Plan Overview

How the master plan is laid out

The DHA Phase 2 master plan separates land into four functional zones so that homes, shops, schools and services sit in the right places, with wide boulevards linking every sector to GT Road and the Islamabad Expressway.

Residential zone

The largest share of land. Plots of 5 Marla, 8 Marla, 10 Marla and 1 Kanal are spread across all nine sectors, with corner and park-facing plots in every block.

Commercial zone

Sector markets in every sector, the Central Boulevard corridor, and the high-value GT Road frontage around Giga Mall. Commercial plots range from 4 Marla to 1 Kanal.

Institutional zone

Land reserved for schools, colleges, clinics and community buildings, including DHA Army Public School campuses in Sectors C, F and G.

Public utilities

Mosques in every sector, parks and green belts, the Sector G graveyard, grid stations, water tanks and tube wells, sewerage and waste management.

Main roads and boulevards

  • Central Boulevard, the main spine running through the middle of Phase 2
  • Jinnah Boulevard, linking Gate 4 on GT Road into the community
  • Iqbal Boulevard, entered from Gate 2 on GT Road
  • GT Road (N-5) frontage on the western and southern side, Gates 1–4
  • Islamabad Expressway (N-80) on the northern and eastern side, Gates 5, 7 and 8
  • Tree-lined sector roads with footpaths on both sides

Sector-by-Sector Guide

Every sector in detail

Plots, high-rises, rates and facilities for each of the nine sectors. Select a sector to see its details:

A

Sector A

Commercial & high-rise hub

Sector A is one of the most mature and commercially active parts of DHA Phase 2. Its fully developed markets, bank branches and high-rise access make it a favourite for buyers who want everything within walking distance, and for investors chasing rental demand from working professionals.

Plots available

  • 10 Marla and 1 Kanal residential plots form the core of the sector
  • 8 Marla plots in selected streets
  • 4 and 5 Marla commercial plots in the sector market
  • Corner and park-facing plots carry a premium of roughly 10–15%

High-rises & apartments

Sector A sits next to the Lignum Tower corridor, giving residents quick access to its studio to 4-bedroom apartments. The sector's commercial market is lined with multi-storey plazas that combine ground-floor shops with offices and apartments on the upper floors, one of the busiest rental pockets in Phase 2.

B

Sector B

Family living & sports

Sector B is built around family life. It is home to one of the largest open green areas in Phase 2, the DHA Tennis Court and a popular food strip, which makes it one of the most in-demand sectors for end users with children.

Plots available

  • 5 Marla plots in selected blocks
  • 10 Marla residential plots
  • 1 Kanal residential plots, many near the family park
  • Commercial plots around the sector shopping mall

High-rises & apartments

Sector B has no tower-style high-rises. Living here is mostly independent houses, with a limited number of apartments above shops in the sector shopping mall and commercial strip, which suits buyers who want a quieter, low-density street.

Sector C

Schools, markets & Lignum Tower

Sector C combines busy commercial markets with a strong cluster of schools, and is home to Lignum Tower, the landmark 20-storey apartment building near the Islamabad Expressway. It is a practical pick for families who prioritise the school run and for apartment investors.

High-rises & apartments

Lignum Tower is the headline high-rise in Sector C: 20 storeys of studio to 4-bedroom luxury apartments with high-speed lifts, standby power, covered parking, a gym, kids' play area, CCTV and intercom. The sector market adds multi-storey plazas with apartments on upper floors.

Sector E

Jacaranda Family Club

Sector E is the recreational heart of DHA Phase 2. It is home to Jacaranda Family Club (JFC), the community's premier leisure destination, and its streets are mostly low-density family homes, which gives the sector a calm, upscale feel.

Sector E is a low-rise, villa-style sector with no high-rise towers. The nearest apartment options are in Sectors A, C and G, a short drive away.

Sector F

Giga Mall & GT Road frontage

Sector F is the most self-contained sector in Phase 2. It sits at Gate 1 on GT Road, right next to Giga Mall, and has its own schools, mosques and commercial area. Its GT Road frontage also holds the most valuable commercial land in the community.

Buyer's Guide

Which sector is right for you?

Families with school-age children

Sectors C, F and G have APS campuses and other schools within walking distance. Sector B adds a large family park and tennis court.

Lifestyle and recreation

Sector E puts Jacaranda Family Club on your doorstep, while Sector F is next to Giga Mall and Sector G has the Central Boulevard food scene.

Budget and first-time buyers

Sectors H and J offer the lowest entry prices, with 5 Marla plots from around 1.5 Cr. Sector D is good value with a full commercial market.

Investors and rental income

Apartments in Sectors A, C and G rent quickly to professionals. GT Road commercial plots in Sector F are the top end for long-term capital growth.

FAQs

Master plan FAQs

How many sectors are in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Nine sectors: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H and J. There is no Sector I.

What is the total area of DHA Phase 2?

About 15,075 Kanals, divided into residential, commercial, institutional and public utility zones.

Which is the most expensive sector in DHA Phase 2?

Sectors A and F generally sit at the top of the range for residential plots, with 1 Kanal plots up to around 6.5 Cr. GT Road facing commercial land in Sector F is the most valuable land in Phase 2.

Which is the most affordable sector?

Sectors H and J, where 5 Marla plots start from around 1.5 Cr and 1 Kanal plots from around 3.5 Cr.

Where are the high-rise apartments in DHA Phase 2?

Mainly Lignum Tower in Sector C, the Central Boulevard corridor in Sector G, and the GT Road commercial belt near Giga Mall in Sector F. Most sector markets also have flats above shops.

Is DHA Phase 2 fully developed?

Yes. All sectors have possession, underground electricity, gas, water, sewerage and internet, and most streets are already built up with houses.
