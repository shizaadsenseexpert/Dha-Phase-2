DHA Phase 2 Islamabad Location

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad sits in Zone 5 of the CDA, in the Kahuta Triangle between GT Road (N-5) and the Islamabad Expressway (N-80). It's one of the easiest addresses in the twin cities to reach from both Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Choose a guide below for maps, distances, routes and what's nearby.
