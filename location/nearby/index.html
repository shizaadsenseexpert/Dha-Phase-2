Places to Visit

Shopping & malls

This is where DHA Phase 2 truly shines — the community and its surroundings are packed with some of the best leisure, dining and entertainment spots in the twin cities.

Giga Mall — Sector F, DHA Phase 2 (0 km — inside Phase 2)

Pakistan's premier shopping and entertainment destination. 200+ brands, Cinepax cinema, an international food court, a kids' play zone, and the upcoming Giga Mall Extension with a rooftop mosque and five-star hotel.

Giga Boutique Mall — Adjacent to Giga Mall

Islamabad's first indoor water park, a skywalk bridge, cafés, and year-round entertainment. A must-visit for families.

Centaurus Mall, Islamabad — 20 km

One of Pakistan's most iconic malls, in Blue Area, Islamabad. Luxury brands, cinema and dining.

Bahria Town Civic Center — 10–12 km

A large commercial and entertainment complex within Bahria Town.

Places to Visit

Recreation & family spots

Jacaranda Family Club (JFC) — Sector E, DHA Phase 2 (inside Phase 2)

DHA Phase 2's crown jewel for recreation. Bowling alleys, tennis courts, swimming pools, a gymnasium, a spa, JFC Cinema, wedding halls, and multiple restaurants — all on a 20-acre estate.

Sector B Family Park — DHA Phase 2 (inside Phase 2)

One of the largest public parks inside Phase 2. Ideal for weekend family outings, morning walks, and kids' play.

Bahria Town Grand Mosque — 10 km

One of the largest mosques in the world, open to visitors — a stunning architectural landmark worth visiting.

Shakarparian Hills & Rose Garden — 22 km

A scenic hilltop park in Islamabad with walking trails, the Pakistan Monument, and panoramic views of the Margalla Hills.

Fatima Jinnah Park (F-9 Park) — 22 km

Islamabad's largest public park — ideal for picnics, jogging, and family outings.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What housing societies are near DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Bahria Town Phase 7, DHA Phase 1, DHA Phase 5, Zaraj Housing Society, Capital Smart City, Canyon Views, and Soan Gardens are the closest communities.

What are the best places to visit near DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Giga Mall, Jacaranda Family Club, Giga Boutique Mall, Bahria Town Grand Mosque, Shakarparian Hills, and F-9 Park are top picks.

Is Centaurus Mall near DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Yes — Centaurus Mall in Blue Area Islamabad is approximately 20 km away, around a 20–25 minute drive via the Islamabad Expressway.

Which hospital is closest to DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Fauji Foundation Hospital (near DHA Phase 1) is approximately 5 km away. Riphah International Hospital and IDC are also within 5–7 km.

Is Capital Smart City near DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Capital Smart City is approximately 15–18 km from DHA Phase 2, accessible via GT Road and the Islamabad Expressway corridor.
