Education

Schools inside and next to Phase 2

Schooling is one of the main reasons families choose DHA Phase 2. The DHA Army Public School system (APS/APSACS) runs campuses inside the community, in Sector C, Sector F and Sector G, and several well-known private school chains are inside Phase 2 or a few minutes away.

Most institutions follow Cambridge O/A Levels or Matric with co-curricular programmes. Nearly every school is within a 5-minute drive, and several are walkable from residential streets, which keeps the school run short and safe inside the gated area.

Healthcare

Clinics in every sector, hospitals close by

Everyday healthcare is available inside every sector of Phase 2. Clinics, diagnostic labs and pharmacies sit in the sector commercial areas, and DHA administration runs a 24-hour ambulance service for residents.

For specialist and emergency care, several major hospitals are a short drive away. Fauji Foundation Hospital near DHA Phase 1 is about 5 km away, Riphah International Hospital and Islamabad Diagnostic Centre (IDC) are within 5–7 km, and Shifa International Hospital is a 15–20 minute drive.

Security

Gated, guarded and patrolled 24/7

Security is the facility most residents value first. Every one of the 7 entry gates is staffed by armed security personnel, and mobile patrol units cover the whole community day and night. CCTV cameras are installed sector by sector across streets and commercial zones.

Security staff carry walkie-talkies for rapid response, and a police post inside Phase 2 adds a law enforcement presence. Well-lit streets make it safe to walk in the evenings, and high-rise buildings such as Lignum Tower add their own building-level CCTV and intercom systems.

Security measures

  • Armed security personnel at all 7 gates
  • 24/7 mobile patrol units
  • Sector-wise CCTV on streets and commercial zones
  • Walkie-talkie-equipped staff for rapid response
  • Police post within Phase 2
  • Well-lit streets for safe evening movement

Legal security

DHA Islamabad operates under the DHA Islamabad Act 2013, passed by the National Assembly. This makes Phase 2 one of the most legally secure housing communities in Pakistan, with transfers handled directly by DHA.

Good to know: Register your household staff and vehicles with DHA security for smooth gate entry

Parks & green areas

Family parks, pocket parks and tree-lined boulevards

DHA Phase 2 is planned around green space. Sector B's Family Park is one of the largest open recreational areas in the community, with a walking track and children's play areas, and Central Park sits near Central Boulevard in Sector G.

Every sector has its own pocket parks, and all the major boulevards have landscaped green belt medians. Footpaths run on both sides of every road, so walking, jogging and cycling are safe throughout Phase 2.

Mosques

At least one in every sector

Every sector in DHA Phase 2 has at least one mosque built for 300 or more worshippers, so prayer is always within walking distance. Sectors A and G each have three mosques, Sector F has two, and Sector B has a central mosque.

Sector F also sits close to the Rooftop Mosque of the Giga Mall Extension, and Sector C has mosques in each of its blocks.

Shopping

Giga Mall plus a market in every sector

Giga Mall, one of the largest malls in the twin cities, sits at the edge of Sector F next to Gate 1 on GT Road. It has 200+ brands, a Cinepax cinema, an international food court and a kids' play zone, and the Giga Mall Extension and Giga Boutique Mall next door add even more retail.

For daily needs, every sector has its own commercial market or marts. Sector A has fully developed commercial markets, Sector C's markets are among the busiest, Sector B has a sector shopping mall, and Sectors H and J have family marts and daily-needs shops.

The community's leisure hub in Sector E

Jacaranda Family Club (JFC) in Sector E is the community's premier leisure destination. It brings together a bowling alley, cinema, tennis courts, swimming pool, spa, gym and fitness centre, restaurants and wedding and event halls in one place.

Outside the club, residents have outdoor badminton and basketball courts, the DHA Tennis Court in Sector B, an outdoor cinema and the entertainment at Giga Mall. High-rise projects such as Lignum Tower and Central Palace Residence add their own gyms and sports facilities.

Utilities

Underground and available around the clock

All main utilities in DHA Phase 2 are fully in place. Electricity runs through an underground supply and is available 24/7, natural gas is connected throughout, and water comes from tube wells and storage tanks with round-the-clock supply.

Fibre and broadband internet are available from Nayatel, PTCL and Transworld, and the sewerage and waste management system is fully functional. Underground cabling also keeps streets free of overhead wires.

Access & roads

GT Road and Islamabad Expressway gates

DHA Phase 2 sits between GT Road and the Islamabad Expressway, with guarded gates on both. Gates 1–4 open onto GT Road for Rawalpindi traffic, and Gates 5 and 7 connect to the Islamabad Expressway for the quickest route into Islamabad.

Inside, wide boulevards such as Central Boulevard, Jinnah Boulevard and Iqbal Boulevard link the sectors, with footpaths on both sides of every road. Rawalpindi is 10–15 minutes away and central Islamabad around 20–25 minutes.

Community services

The everyday services that keep Phase 2 running

Beyond the headline facilities, DHA Phase 2 has the services a complete town needs. A fire-fighting facility serves the community, waste collection and sewerage are fully functional, and a graveyard is located in Sector G.

For visitors and students, Sector H has guest houses for short stays and the ISR Boys Hostel. Building management in the high-rises handles lifts, power backup, cleaning and security for apartment owners.

  • 24-hour DHA ambulance service

FAQs

Facilities FAQs

What facilities are available in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Giga Mall, Jacaranda Family Club, APS and top private schools, clinics, parks, mosques, underground utilities, fibre internet, banks and armed 24/7 security, all within the community.

Which schools are in DHA Phase 2?

DHA Army Public School (APS/APSACS) has campuses in Sectors C, F and G. Roots International and Headstart are inside or next to Phase 2, and The City School and Forbels are a 5-minute drive.

Is there a hospital inside DHA Phase 2?

Every sector has clinics, labs and pharmacies, and DHA runs a 24-hour ambulance service. The nearest hospitals are Fauji Foundation Hospital (about 5 km), Riphah International Hospital and IDC (5–7 km).

Is DHA Phase 2 safe?

Yes. Armed guards staff all 7 gates, mobile patrols run 24/7, CCTV covers streets and commercial zones sector by sector, and there is a police post inside Phase 2.

What is at Jacaranda Family Club?

JFC in Sector E has a bowling alley, cinema, tennis courts, swimming pool, spa, gym, restaurants and wedding and event halls.

Are electricity, gas and water available 24/7?

Yes. Electricity is supplied underground and available 24/7, gas is connected throughout, and water comes from tube wells and tanks around the clock.

Facility details are based on the latest available information and can change as DHA adds or upgrades services. Confirm timings, memberships and admissions directly with each institution or DHA administration.
