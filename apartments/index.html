High-Rise Living

Apartments in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad

DHA Phase 2 has become one of Islamabad's key high-rise apartment corridors. From the 20-storey Lignum Tower to Central Boulevard projects with instalment plans and mixed-use buildings next to Giga Mall, apartments here combine DHA security and infrastructure with a lower entry price than houses. This guide covers every high-rise building, sector by sector, with amenities and 2025 prices.

Why Apartments

Why buy an apartment in DHA Phase 2?

Apartments let buyers get into a fully developed DHA community for a fraction of the price of a house, with less upkeep and strong rental demand.

Lower entry price

Apartments start from around 72 Lac, compared with about 1.5 Cr for the cheapest 5 Marla plot.

DHA security

Gated sectors, armed guards at all 7 gates, 24/7 patrols and CCTV, plus building-level security.

Low maintenance

Building management handles lifts, power backup, cleaning and security, ideal for overseas owners.

Rental demand

Professionals and small families keep demand high, with estimated yields of 5–7% a year.

Amenities

Towers like Lignum offer gyms, play areas, covered parking and backup power that most houses lack.

Instalment options

Some projects, such as Central Palace Residence, offer developer instalment plans.

C

Lignum Tower

Sector C, near the Islamabad Expressway

Lignum Tower is the best-known high-rise in DHA Phase 2 and one of the most recognisable buildings on the Islamabad Expressway. Its 20 storeys hold studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4-bedroom luxury apartments, making it the most complete serviced apartment address inside DHA.

Residents get the security of a DHA sector plus the convenience of a managed building: lifts, standby power, covered parking and on-site amenities. The surrounding Sector C has an APS campus, several schools and busy markets, and Gates 5 and 7 on the Expressway put Islamabad within easy reach.

Central Palace Residence

Central Boulevard corridor (Sector G)

Central Palace Residence sits in Phase 2's central corridor along Central Boulevard, the busiest road inside the community. It offers a range of apartment layouts with shops, restaurants, gyms and sports facilities within the project.

Its biggest draw for many buyers is the payment structure: instalment plans are available through the developer, which is rare in a mature community where plots trade only on a cash or resale basis. International food brands, banks and an APS campus are on the doorstep.

Giga Mall complex & GT Road high-rises

Sector F, next to Gate 1 on GT Road

The Giga Mall complex and the GT Road commercial belt in Sector F form the most commercial skyline in Phase 2. Multi-storey buildings here mix retail on the lower floors with offices and apartment floors above, and the Giga Mall Extension has added more vertical space.

Living here means shopping, dining, a cinema and family entertainment are an elevator ride or short walk away, with direct access to GT Road through Gate 1 for Rawalpindi and Bahria Town.

A

Sector A: High-rise hub

Commercial & High-Rise Hub

Sector A is one of the strongest apartment addresses in Phase 2. Its market plazas are full of upper-floor flats that rent quickly to professionals, and Lignum Tower is right next door for buyers who want a serviced building with lifts, backup power and covered parking. Expect higher maintenance charges in the tower, but also faster tenancy and firmer resale values than in quieter sectors.

Sector A sits next to the Lignum Tower corridor, which gives residents quick access to its studio to 4-bedroom apartments without leaving the area. Lignum Tower itself rises 20 storeys and offers high-speed lifts, standby power, covered parking, a gym and CCTV security.

Inside the sector, the commercial market is lined with multi-storey plazas. These buildings usually have shops on the ground floor and offices or apartments on the upper floors, which makes them one of the busiest rental pockets in Phase 2 for singles, couples and small families.

High-rise at a glance

Sector B: Limited apartments

Family Living & Sports

Sector B is a house-first sector. The only apartments are a small number of flats above shops in the sector mall and food strip. They are compact and affordable, and the big draw is living a short walk from the large family park and DHA Tennis Court. Supply is limited, so good units are snapped up quickly.

Sector B has no tower-style high-rise buildings. Living here is almost entirely independent houses, which is a big part of its appeal for families who want privacy and a quiet street.

A limited number of flats are available above shops in the sector shopping mall and food strip. These suit students, young couples and small families who want to live near the park on a smaller budget. For full high-rise living, Lignum Tower and the Central Boulevard projects are a short drive away.

C

Sector C: Signature tower

Lignum Tower, Schools & Markets

Sector C is the most important high-rise sector in DHA Phase 2 because it is home to Lignum Tower. The 20-storey building offers studio to 4-bedroom apartments with a full set of amenities, and it is the most established serviced apartment address inside DHA. The APS campus and nearby schools make it practical for families, and Expressway access suits Islamabad commuters.

Lignum Tower is the headline high-rise in Sector C. It offers 20 storeys of studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4-bedroom luxury apartments with high-speed lifts, standby power backup, covered car parking, a gymnasium, a kids' play area, CCTV security and an intercom system.

Upper-floor units enjoy open views across Phase 2, while lower floors are popular with families who want easy lift access. Apartments here are in demand from overseas Pakistanis, professionals and small families, which supports steady rental income.

Beyond the tower, the sector market has multi-storey plazas with apartments above shops for buyers on a smaller budget.

Buyer's Checklist

Before you buy an apartment

  • Confirm the project has an approved NOC from DHA Islamabad
  • Check the developer's track record and completed projects
  • Get the exact block, floor, unit number and covered area in writing
  • Ask for the full payment schedule and any late-payment penalties
  • Find out monthly maintenance charges and what they cover
  • Check parking allocation and power backup capacity
  • Visit the site and inspect construction quality and finishes
  • Verify the transfer process and fees with DHA before final payment

FAQs

Apartment FAQs

Which is the best apartment building in DHA Phase 2?

Lignum Tower in Sector C is the most established, with 20 storeys, studio to 4-bedroom units and full amenities. Central Palace Residence is popular for its instalment plans.

What is the cheapest apartment in DHA Phase 2?

Flats above shops in sector market plazas start from around 65 Lac, and 1-bedroom apartments in projects from around 72 Lac.

Can I buy an apartment in DHA Phase 2 on instalments?

Yes, some projects such as Central Palace Residence offer developer instalment plans. Plots and houses trade on a resale basis.

Which sectors have no apartments?

Sectors E, H and J are low-rise with no apartments. Sector B has only a few flats above shops.

What rental yield can I expect from an apartment?

Estimated at 5–7% a year, with the strongest demand in Sectors A, C and G.

Prices are indicative 2025 market ranges and change with floor, view, finish and project. Confirm the latest price, approvals and possession status with the developer, DHA and a registered dealer before buying.
