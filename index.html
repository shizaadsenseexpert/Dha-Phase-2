Overview

A self-sufficient urban neighbourhood

DHA Phase 2 Islamabad is one of the most fully developed, highly populated and commercially vibrant residential communities in the twin cities of Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Developed by the Defence Housing Authority (DHA) and built by Habib Rafiq (Pvt.) Limited, this phase was originally launched in 1992 and has grown into a self-sufficient urban neighbourhood unlike any other in the region.

Spanning approximately 15,075 Kanals, DHA Phase 2 is divided into 9 residential sectors — from Sector A through Sector J, excluding I — each fully developed and home to thousands of families. It is legally approved under the Presidential Ordinance 2005 and the DHA Islamabad Act, passed by the National Assembly of Pakistan, making it one of the most legally secure property investment destinations in the country.

Location

Positioned in the Kahuta Triangle

DHA Phase 2 sits in Zone 5 of the Capital Development Authority (CDA), between two of the twin cities' most critical road arteries — the Islamabad Expressway (N-80) and GT Road (N-5). Residents reach central Rawalpindi in 10–15 minutes and the heart of Islamabad in 15–20 minutes.

Landmarks

Nearby landmarks & attractions

Giga Mall

On GT Road at Gate 1, Sector F — one of Pakistan's largest shopping & entertainment destinations with 200+ brands, Cinepax cinema, food courts and a kids' play area. The Giga Mall Extension adds a five-star hotel, rooftop mosque and expanded retail; the Giga Boutique Mall brings Islamabad's first indoor water park and skywalk bridge.

Jacaranda Family Club (JFC)

A 20-acre luxury club in Sector E — the lifestyle centrepiece of DHA Phase 2, with bowling alleys, tennis courts, swimming pools, a gymnasium, a spa, JFC Cinema, wedding halls and restaurants.

Lignum Tower

A 20-storey high-rise in Sector C offering studio to 4-bedroom luxury apartments, with high-speed elevators, covered parking, gym, prayer area and CCTV security.

Central Park & Central Boulevard

Shared between Sectors F and G — the busiest commercial roundabout in Phase 2, lined with McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Dasterkhwan and multiple brand cafés.

APS School (DHA Army Public School)

One of the most prominent educational institutions in the phase, with branches in Sector F and other sectors.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is DHA Phase 2 Islamabad a good place to live?

Yes — it's a fully developed, gated community with top schools, hospitals, Giga Mall, Jacaranda Club, and 24/7 security. One of the best addresses in the twin cities.

What is the price range of properties in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Residential plots start from PKR 1.5 Crore (5 Marla) and go up to PKR 6.5 Crore (1 Kanal). Apartments begin around PKR 72 Lac and commercial plots from PKR 4 Crore onwards.

Is DHA Phase 2 Islamabad legally safe to invest in?

Yes — it operates under the DHA Islamabad Act 2013, passed by the National Assembly, making it one of the most legally secure housing communities in Pakistan.

What facilities are available in DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Giga Mall, Jacaranda Family Club, APS and top private schools, clinics, parks, mosques, underground utilities, fibre internet, and armed 24/7 security — all within the community.

How do I access DHA Phase 2 Islamabad?

Via 7 gates — Gates 5 & 7 connect to Islamabad Expressway, Gates 1–4 open onto GT Road. Rawalpindi is 10–15 mins away, Islamabad around 20–25 mins.
